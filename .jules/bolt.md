## 2025-05-18 - Fast-path ZIP Normalization & Parcel Quote Caching in `zones.py` and `parcel_mock.py`
**Learning:** `normalize_zip5` and `mock_zone_id` in `unie_cortex/network/zones.py` and `mock_parcel_quote_usd` in `unie_cortex/network/parcel_mock.py` were executing uncached regex substitutions and quote calculations on every parcel evaluation during scenario comparisons. Fast-path string checks (skipping `re.sub` for standard 5-digit, ZIP+4, and 9-digit formats) combined with `@functools.lru_cache` and returning dictionary copies reduced scenario comparison latency from 9.24ms to 2.87ms per call (~3.22x speedup).
**Action:** When working on rate shopping, scenario evaluation, or zone lookups, leverage `@lru_cache` on deterministic lookup functions and ensure return dicts are shallow-copied to prevent shared state mutation.
