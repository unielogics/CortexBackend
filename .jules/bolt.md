## 2026-07-18 - Optimized Zip-to-Contiguous State Mapping with LRU Cache
**Learning:** Geographic lookup functions (like `nearest_contiguous_state_for_zip3`) that clean zip inputs, perform coordinate translation (e.g. `_zip5_to_latlon` with jitter), and calculate haversine distance to 48 states on every call are highly computationally expensive (O(N) distance checks and trigonometry calculations). Since the space of inputs (ZIP3 codes) is small (at most 1000 unique values) and the mappings/coordinates are static, caching the entire space with `@functools.lru_cache(maxsize=1024)` yields a massive speedup (~116x faster, reducing 10k calls from ~964ms to under 9ms).
**Action:** Always identify deterministic functions with small input cardinalities (such as ZIP3 prefixes or categories) that perform expensive calculations or lookups inside loops, and apply `@lru_cache` to eliminate redundant work.
