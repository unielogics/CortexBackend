## 2026-07-31 - Spherical Law of Cosines Maximum vs Haversine Minimum
**Learning:** For nearest-neighbor geographic queries on a sphere, physical distance computation (e.g., via the Haversine formula) is not required. Since the arccosine function is strictly monotonically decreasing, maximizing the cosine of the central angle via the Spherical Law of Cosines is mathematically identical to minimizing the physical distance. Pre-computing coordinates into radians and their sines/cosines at the module level allows us to avoid all square roots and inverse trigonometric functions in the loop, providing a ~7.7x cold-path speedup.
**Action:** When finding the closest coordinate among a fixed/static set of geographic points, pre-compute coordinates as `(lat_rad, lon_rad, sin(lat_rad), cos(lat_rad))` and find the candidate that maximizes `sin_lat1 * sin_lat2 + cos_lat1 * cos_lat2 * cos(lon2 - lon1)`.
