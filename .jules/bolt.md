## 2026-07-25 - Fast-path parsing of order dates for year extraction
**Learning:** Extracting years from order date strings using generic `re` matching and sequential `datetime.strptime` checks inside loops is extremely slow. Micro-benchmarks show that `strptime` parsing of slash-delimited date formats (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`) takes over ~2.66s per 100k iterations, whereas introducing simple character checks (`s[:4].isdigit()` or `s[2] == '/' and s[5] == '/'`) and string slices allows bypassing this overhead entirely. This fast-path reduces the cost of the common-path parsing to ~0.08s (over a 30x speedup).
**Action:** Always prefer indexing/slicing checks on format characters (like dashes, slashes, or Ts) in hot loops parsing strings before resorting to complex regexes or standard library datetime-parsing fallbacks.
