## 2026-03-31 - [LRU Caching and Math Optimization for Transport Miles]
**Learning:** In transport network calculations (`compute_transport_miles_v1`), repeated geodesic and road pair queries across ZIP centroids occur frequently. Decorating `geodesic_miles_zip5` and `road_miles_zip5_pair` with `@functools.lru_cache(maxsize=16384)` and streamlining `haversine_miles` mathematical operations (avoiding `map()` list allocation and exponentiation `** 2`) reduced transportation matrix calculation latency from 0.2916 ms to 0.0585 ms per call (~5.2x speedup / 80% reduction in latency).
**Action:** When computing O/D matrix calculations or routing distances between discrete spatial proxies (like ZIP centroids), apply `functools.lru_cache` to pair distance lookup functions and optimize inner math calculations to avoid scalar allocation overhead.
