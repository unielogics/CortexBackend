## 2025-10-24 - [Regressions in Codebase Memory and Geographic Distance Optimizations]
**Learning:** Documented optimizations from previous sessions or documentation (like the caching and radian pre-computations for `nearest_contiguous_state_for_zip3` in `zip_geo.py`) can sometimes be missing or reverted due to branch discrepancy or codebase state. Always verify the current state of files before assuming pre-existing optimizations are in place. Caching repetitive lookups with `functools.lru_cache` and pre-converting coordinates to radians for mathematical distance loops (e.g. Haversine) significantly cuts down overhead by over 15x on unique requests and over 700x on repeated requests in batch-style loops.
**Action:** Always run a quick baseline run/benchmark of the target functions first, inspect the actual codebase file, and verify against memory. Pre-compute constant parameters outside of high-frequency loops.
